Anime Card RNG — Game System Overhaul

Implemented modules

1. Dynamic battle matchmaking
- 1v1 enemy final power targets 0.92x–1.22x of player power.
- 4v4 enemy final team power targets 0.85x–1.30x of player team power.
- Scaling is applied to final combat stats so high-level cards do not create runaway opponent inflation.
- Existing turn order, mutations, weather effects, 25% defeat loss chance, and Battle Again flow are preserved.

2. Salvage-only cash economy
- Normal rolls no longer award instant cash.
- Manual Salvage 1 Copy removes exactly one selected card+mutation copy and pays its salvage value.
- Salvage Extra Duplicates keeps one copy of each distinct mutation variant and salvages extras.
- Auto-Salvage can match Normal/No Mutation, individual mutations, or a rarity threshold. Matching new rolls convert directly to cash instead of entering storage.
- Existing currency is preserved during save/load migration.

3. Shared card level and EXP
- Progress is stored by base card ID in `cardProgress`, shared by every mutation variant of that card.
- Level growth is +0.75% base HP/ATK per level.
- EXP requirement is 100 × rarity multiplier × current level.
- Collection and active Battle Arena cards show level and EXP progress.

4. Mobile battle UI
- Battle setup, active arena, and result views support vertical touch scrolling on narrow screens.
- Safe-area padding and WebKit momentum scrolling are included.
- Battle logs and fighter status remain accessible on small viewports.

Persistence
- Save schema advanced to v5. Existing LocalStorage is migrated in place.
- Legacy `cardLevels`/`levels` migrate to `cardProgress`.
- Legacy `autoDelete`/`autoSalvageSettings` migrate to `autoSalvage`.
- Card inventory/mutation normalization remains active.
