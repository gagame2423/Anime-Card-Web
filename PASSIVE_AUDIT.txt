SCOPE / FIXES
- Full roster: 217 canonical cards; exact category refresh for all 217.
  cards.js non-category fields compared byte-for-value against previous build.
- Sins/Tensura/Bleach/Tower200: one shared plain descriptor observer registry;
  no layered Proxy setters or Reflect.set cycles. Hook labels install once.
- All 9 *_passives.js: idempotent script guards and per-API factory caches.
  Other chained skill factories retain isolated state and are cached per API.
- ensureRuntime re-entry guard; per-field setter guard and bounded callback depth.
  try/finally releases guards even after a blocked transform or thrown callback.
- damageUnit and champion hit guard source re-entry plus depth > 2. Sequential
  follow-ups remain valid after the previous resolution unwinds.
- Malevolent King: follow-up slashes no longer open another full nested volley.
  Auto Chess has a shared 64-dispatch budget per action, plus local chain caps.
  This prevents multiplicative nesting even when each individual loop is bounded.
- Combat units/stats/runtime and match Relics are isolated from permanent inventory.
  The copied-passive virtual view remains private to its copy scope; units do not
  acquire stacked Proxy observers. No global per-unit event listeners are added.
- Deterministic native grid scheduler, event order and seeded random source remain
  in use. Worker has its own module/runtime instances and cannot write localStorage.
- Shield/heal/HP-loss/kill measurements feed match-local telemetry. One official
  battle per tactician/round; ghost opponents do not duplicate the defender record.
- Augments are match-local: 3 Armories, slot rerolls once, real bot selections,
  ISO UTC weekly pool fixed at match creation, capped Gold credited centrally.
